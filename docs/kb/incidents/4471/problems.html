The three problems

Parser. The shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants all write the day first, so day and month swap whenever both are 12 or less: a reminder for 7 April goes out on 4 July. These reports come through the app, by email, and through the booking-v2 form.

booking-v3 hint. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People follow the hint and type the month first, and the form then reads the date day first. The symptom is the same swap, so subject-line merging put these reports with the parser ones. The fix is in the form, not in the parser.

One-day shift. A handful of reports describe a date that moved by exactly one day: Tuesday showed as Monday, one meeting landed on Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto. That is not a swap. It points to a time-zone conversion. The desk already read these as one-day moves (desk-notes.json), but the cause table on #4471 still counts them under the parser.

Why one ticket hid three

  • Merging went by subject line (decision 0001). Different causes share the same subjects, so they landed in one ticket.
  • Intake tags come from the reporter's browser. The column "intake tag matches" above shows how often the tag agrees with the cause: often for the parser, rarely for the hint.
  • The export pages overlap, so some reports appear twice. Counts here are by unique report id.

What the desk can do next

  • Before merging into a known ticket, check the cause with triage, not only the subject.
  • The parser fix has shipped and booking-v3 is gone, so a new report of a wrong date is most likely the one-day shift. File it on the shift ticket, not on #4471.
